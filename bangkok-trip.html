⭐ Base Camp for All 4 Nights

W22 by Burasari @ Chinatown

Located on Mittraphan Road (near 22 July Circle) in vibrant Chinatown. A stylish lifestyle boutique hotel that keeps you in the cultural pulse of Bangkok with zero hotel hopping.

🚇 Transit Access

  • 6 min walk to MRT Wat Mangkon & MRT Hua Lamphong (Blue Line)
  • Direct underground line to:
    • Sam Yan (Banthat Thong food street)
    • Sukhumvit / Asok (BTS interchange)
    • Chatuchak Park (Weekend market)
    • Sanam Chai (Grand Palace / Old Town)
    • Phetchaburi (Airport Rail Link link)

🏨 Hotel Amenities & Tips

  • 7-story sunlit atrium, communal loft with pool table & rooftop bar / terrace
  • Late check-in: Arriving ~23:30 Wed night. Notify hotel in advance.
  • Check-out: Sun 11 Oct by 13:00. Leave luggage at front desk until 15:30.
  • Atmospheric hipster cafe vibe, laundry corner, and street food steps away on Mittraphan & Yaowarat.

Bangkok Street Smart Tips

🛕 Temple Etiquette

  • Dress code: Shoulders and knees MUST be covered at Grand Palace and Wat Arun. No sheer fabric or ripped jeans.
  • Slip-on shoes are convenient as shoes must be removed before entering temple viharas.

⚠️ Scam Prevention

  • If any tuk-tuk driver or stranger near Grand Palace says "The temple is closed today for a Buddhist holiday", it is a 100% scam to steer you to gem shops. Walk straight to the official gate.

🚕 Rush Hour Traffic

  • Sukhumvit (Thu 17:30–19:30): Never take Grab from Emsphere/Phrom Phong to Banthat Thong during rush hour. Take BTS → MRT instead.
  • Rainy season: October evenings can see quick showers. Keep an umbrella in your day pack.

🎉 Special Events

  • BKK Comics Art Festival: Oct 6–18 at BACC (3rd Floor).
  • Yaowarat Vegetarian Festival: Opening night on Saturday, 10 Oct right on your street!
