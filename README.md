## ✈️ Trip Summary
* **Dates:** Wednesday, 7 Oct – Sunday, 11 Oct 2026 (5 days / 4 nights)
* **Base Camp:** W22 by Burasari, Chinatown (Mittraphan Rd / 22 July Circle)
* **Key Stops:**
  * **Wed 7 Oct:** Arrival late (22:00 BKK) & W22 by Burasari check-in
  * **Thu 8 Oct:** Ari (Khao Soi), Victory Monument (Boat Noodles), Siam & BACC, CentralWorld, Emsphere, Banthat Thong (Jeh O Chula), Jodd Fairs (Leng Saeb)
  * **Fri 9 Oct:** On Lok Yun breakfast, Grand Palace, Wang Lang Market, Wat Arun, Old Town food walk, Makro Sathorn, Sunset Chao Phraya boat, ICONSIAM
  * **Sat 10 Oct:** Suriya Coffee (facing Giant Buddha), Talat Phlu, Or Tor Kor & Chatuchak Weekend Market, Union Mall, MOCA Bangkok, Yaowarat Vegetarian Festival & Seafood
  * **Sun 11 Oct:** Khlong Lat Mayom Floating Market, TCDC & Talat Noi / Songwat heritage walk, Chinatown check-out, Suvarnabhumi Airport departure (19:00 flight)

## 📱 Features of the Web App
* **Mobile-First & Offline Capable:** Ticks and notes save automatically to your phone's local storage.
* **Sync & Share:** Tap **"Share progress"** to send your current checked stops and notes to your travel companion via a shareable link.
* **Interactive Navigation:** Direct Google Maps pins, Google Photos visual previews, and public transit directions (MRT / BTS / Boat / Grab) for every stop.
* **Curated Checklists:** Tracks 19 must-try dishes, 83 food & drink gems, 25 shopping spots, and 27 sights.
